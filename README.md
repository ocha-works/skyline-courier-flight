Skyline Courier — a courier flight game. Fly a light cargo plane over a river valley in a 3D chase view: thread 8 sky rings, drop a parcel on the market square, or put her down on the Isle of Gulls strip. Throttle, bank and pitch discipline, fuel and hull management, and three missions with different goals decide every sortie. Keyboard and mouse, no build step, no network.

## Run it

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8080 --directory game
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. W/S throttle, A/D bank, mouse steers pitch, G gear, F flaps, P pause. Press 1, 2 or 3 on the menu to pick a mission.
